% ── COMMERCIAL MODEL ─────────────────────────────────────────
\section*{Commercial Model}

The AutoCommerce Platform is already built and live in production.
You are not funding development --- you are licensing a working
product. Pricing is made up of a \textbf{one-time setup fee}
(onboarding, configuration, and go-live) and a
\textbf{monthly platform fee} (hosting, maintenance, security
updates, and support). Any feature not already implemented
can be requested as a separate quoted add-on.

% ── NEGOTIATION ───────────────────────────────────────────────
\section*{Open to Negotiation}

We understand that launching a new digital channel is a significant
decision, and we want to make this partnership work for both sides.
The prices above are our standard rates based on comparable
specialised automotive SaaS platforms and agency benchmarks ---
substantially below the cost of a custom agency build of equivalent
scope (typically \$30{,}000--\$80{,}000+).

\medskip
\noindent We are open to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Phased payment structures} --- spreading the setup
        fee over the first 2--3 months.
  \item \textbf{Trial period} --- a reduced-rate first 3 months
        to allow Flamingo to validate the platform before full
        commitment.
  \item \textbf{Annual billing discount} --- pay 12 months of
        the monthly fee upfront and receive 2 months free
        (approximately 17\% saving).
  \item \textbf{Custom scoping} --- if the standard packages do
        not exactly fit your needs, we are happy to build a
        bespoke package around your catalogue size, feature
        requirements, and budget.
\end{itemize}

% ── PAYMENT TERMS ───────────────────────────────────────────────
\section*{Payment Terms}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Setup Fee:} 50\% on contract signature;
        50\% on go-live confirmation.
  \item \textbf{Monthly Fee:} invoiced monthly in advance from
        the go-live date.
  \item \textbf{Payment method:} international bank transfer
        (SWIFT/IBAN), PayPal, or Wise.
  \item \textbf{Currency:} United States Dollar (USD).
  \item \textbf{Contract term:} minimum 12 months.
  \item \textbf{Taxes:} prices are exclusive of any applicable
        local taxes or withholding taxes.
\end{itemize}

% ── NOT INCLUDED ─────────────────────────────────────────────
\section*{What Is Not Included}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item Third-party payment gateway transaction fees.
  \item Domain name registration or renewal.
  \item Physical delivery or logistics costs.
  \item Product photography, copywriting, or translation.
  \item Custom features not listed above (quoted separately).
\end{itemize}
