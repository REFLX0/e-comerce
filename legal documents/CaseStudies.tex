\section*{Case Study 1: SpecPart (formerly KiosqueTN)\\
  \small\normalfont\color{subaccent}Lubricants \& Motor Oil
  E-Commerce --- Tunisia \hfill \textit{2024--Present}}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Industry:} Distribution of lubricants, motor oils,
        filters, and automotive products.
  \item \textbf{Location:} Tunisia --- nationwide delivery to all
        governorates (states).
  \item \textbf{Client type:} Regional automotive product distributor
        serving both retail consumers and professional mechanics.
  \item \textbf{Live deployment:} \texttt{kiosquetn.dev} (live and
        verifiable).
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Challenge}

SpecPart's existing online presence had two critical gaps: customers
could not easily determine which oil or filter was compatible with
their specific vehicle, leading to frequent wrong orders and product
returns. Furthermore, the internal team had no structured tooling to
manage orders, track stock, run promotions, or handle customer
support --- all of which were managed manually.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution Deployed}

AutoCommerce Engineering deployed the full AutoCommerce Platform under
the brand at \texttt{kiosquetn.dev}, in French and English:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Vehicle-to-parts compatibility finder} --- customers
        select their vehicle (make $\rightarrow$ model $\rightarrow$
        engine) or search by technical characteristics. Only
        compatible lubricants and filters are shown.
  \item \textbf{Full admin suite} --- order management with PDF
        invoice export, catalogue and inventory control, coupon and
        promotion management, shipping zone configuration, customer
        management, review moderation, support ticket inbox, and
        analytics dashboard.
  \item \textbf{Customer portal} --- order history with status
        tracking, wishlist, delivery addresses, security settings,
        and after-sales support ticket submission.
  \item \textbf{Managed cloud hosting} on Oracle Cloud VM, with
        NGINX, Docker Compose, GitHub Actions CI/CD, SSL, and
        automated daily database backups.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Timeline}

Delivered from first commit to live production deployment ---
including hosting setup, SSL configuration, security hardening,
and database backups --- by a two-engineer team.
