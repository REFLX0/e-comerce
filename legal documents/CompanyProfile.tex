\section{Executive Summary}

AutoCommerce Engineering is a specialist software engineering studio
focused on one vertical: \textbf{enterprise-grade e-commerce for the
automotive industry}. We design, build, and operate white-label online
stores that allow distributors of lubricants, motor oils, and auto
parts to sell online under their own brand and domain.

Our flagship product --- the \textbf{AutoCommerce Platform} --- is not
a prototype. It is \emph{live in production} today, powering the
Tunisian storefront \texttt{kiosquetn.dev}, and it ships out of the box
with a \textbf{vehicle-to-parts compatibility finder}, a full admin
suite, advanced inventory management, and customer portals.

Because the software is already developed and battle-tested, we can
launch a fully tailored storefront for Flamingo Car Care Tech Ltd.\ at
a fraction of the cost and timeline of any custom-build agency.

% ── SECTION 4 ───────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Products and Services}

\subsection{AutoCommerce Platform (White-Label)}

A complete, branded online store for automotive product distributors.
Delivered as a managed, hosted solution under the client's own domain.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Storefront:} responsive, SEO-ready, bilingual (English
        + any second language), with product catalogue, search, filters,
        product gallery, and technical specifications.
  \item \textbf{Vehicle-to-Parts Compatibility Finder:} customers
        select their vehicle (brand $\rightarrow$ model $\rightarrow$
        engine) or search by technical characteristics (cylinders,
        power, fuel type). Only compatible products are displayed ---
        eliminating wrong orders and returns.
  \item \textbf{Admin Suite:} full order lifecycle management, product
        and catalogue management, inventory and stock alerts, coupon
        and promotion engine, shipping zone configuration, customer
        management, review moderation, support ticket inbox, analytics
        dashboard, and site settings.
  \item \textbf{Customer Portals:} order history with live status,
        wishlist, delivery addresses, security settings, and
        after-sales support tickets.
  \item \textbf{Payments:} cash-on-delivery as standard; payment badge
        support for major card networks and local payment methods.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Implementation \& Launch Service}

End-to-end onboarding: brand configuration, vehicle database setup,
product catalogue import, domain and hosting provisioning, and staff
training. Delivered and managed entirely by our team.

\subsection{Hosting \& Managed Infrastructure}

Cloud VM hosting with NGINX reverse proxy, Docker Compose stack,
automated daily database backups, error tracking, and health
monitoring --- included in the monthly platform fee.

\subsection{Ongoing Support \& Maintenance}

Post-launch support: bug fixes, security patches, platform updates,
feature enhancements, and dedicated email or WhatsApp support
channels. Available across Starter, Business, and Enterprise tiers.
