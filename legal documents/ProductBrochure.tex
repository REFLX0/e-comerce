\section{Overview}

AutoCommerce is an \textbf{enterprise-grade, white-label e-commerce
platform built exclusively for the automotive industry}. It is
designed for distributors of lubricants, motor oils, filters, and
auto parts who want to sell online under their own brand.

The platform is not a mock-up or a proof-of-concept. It is
\textbf{live in production} today, powering the Tunisian storefront
\texttt{kiosquetn.dev}. This means your tailored storefront can launch
in \textbf{4--6 weeks}, not 6--12 months, at a fraction of the cost
of a custom agency build --- with zero build risk.

\medskip
\noindent This brochure outlines every feature your team will have
access to from day one.

% ── SECTION 2 ───────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Key Features}

\subsection{1. Vehicle-to-Parts Compatibility Finder}

The core differentiator of the AutoCommerce Platform. Customers
always buy the right oil or part for their car --- eliminating wrong
orders, reducing returns, and building buyer confidence.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Four vehicle categories:} Automobile, Motorcycle,
        Heavy Duty (trucks \& light commercial vehicles), and
        Agricultural (tractors \& machinery).
  \item \textbf{Search by vehicle:} brand $\rightarrow$ model
        $\rightarrow$ engine variant, e.g.\ Toyota Land Cruiser ---
        4.0 V6 Petrol.
  \item \textbf{Search by technical specification:} number of
        cylinders, power output (kW/hp), and fuel type --- for
        customers who do not know their exact engine code.
  \item \textbf{Guaranteed compatibility results:} only products
        validated against the selected vehicle are displayed.
  \item \textbf{Extendable vehicle database:} pre-loaded with
        popular makes and models; fully configurable for any
        market, including Nigerian vehicle types and African
        market brands.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2. White-Label Storefront}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item 100\% your brand: your logo, colours, domain, and identity.
  \item Fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
  \item Product catalogue with search, smart filters, sorting, and
        pagination.
  \item Product pages with image gallery, technical spec table,
        related products, and customer reviews.
  \item Bilingual by default (English + any second language).
  \item SEO-optimised product, category, and brand pages
        (structured metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{4. Inventory Management}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item Real-time stock levels tracked per product and SKU.
  \item Low-stock threshold alerts surfaced directly in the admin
        dashboard.
  \item Stock quantities automatically decremented on confirmed
        order and reconciled on cancellation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{5. Customer Portal}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item Order history with live status tracking per order.
  \item Wishlist of saved products.
  \item Profile management, delivery addresses, and saved delivery
        regions.
  \item Security settings: password change and active-session
        management.
  \item After-sales support tickets linked to specific orders.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{6. Security \& Infrastructure}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item JWT authentication stored in HttpOnly cookies (XSS-proof).
  \item bcrypt password hashing (industry standard).
  \item Helmet.js security headers on every API response.
  \item NGINX rate limiting on all API routes (10 req/s general,
        5 req/min on authentication endpoints).
  \item Prisma parameterised queries (SQL-injection proof).
  \item Cloud VM hosting with NGINX reverse proxy.
  \item Automated daily PostgreSQL backups with off-site storage.
  \item Sentry error tracking and \texttt{/api/health} monitoring
        endpoint.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{7. Payment Support}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Cash on delivery} as the default payment method
        (standard for most African and Middle Eastern markets).
  \item Payment badge display for VISA, Mastercard, and popular
        local payment gateways.
  \item Additional payment gateway integrations available as
        optional add-ons (quoted separately).
\end{itemize}

\section{How We Launch Your Storefront}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=6pt]
  \item \textbf{Discovery \& Configuration (Week 1--2):} we
        configure your storefront with your brand identity
        (logo, colours, domain), your vehicle database (makes,
        models, engine types relevant to your market), shipping
        zones (states / regions), and payment setup.
  \item \textbf{Catalogue Import (Week 2--3):} your product data
        is uploaded and mapped to vehicle compatibility. We provide
        guidance and tooling to make this fast.
  \item \textbf{Training \& Review (Week 3--4):} your operations
        team receives hands-on training on the admin suite. A
        full review session is held before go-live.
  \item \textbf{Go Live (Week 4--6):} the storefront, admin, and
        customer portal are launched on your live domain, with
        hosting, SSL, backups, and monitoring all active.
  \item \textbf{Post-Launch Support:} ongoing support, security
        updates, and feature enhancements included in the monthly
        platform fee.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Why AutoCommerce for Flamingo Car Care Tech Ltd.}

Flamingo Car Care Tech Ltd.\ distributes over 32 automotive care and
maintenance product lines across Africa, selling through distributors,
auto dealers, supermarkets, and car-wash businesses. An owned
e-commerce channel would allow Flamingo to:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Sell directly to end consumers} --- vehicle owners,
        mechanics, and detailing professionals --- without relying
        solely on third-party retailers.
  \item \textbf{Guarantee product authenticity} --- one of the most
        significant purchase concerns in the African automotive
        aftermarket.
  \item \textbf{Enable vehicle-guided purchasing} --- customers
        select their car first, then see only compatible Flamingo
        products (engine oils, coolants, additives, filters).
  \item \textbf{Manage and scale inventory} across warehouse
        locations with real-time stock visibility.
  \item \textbf{Run promotions and loyalty campaigns} through the
        integrated coupon and promotion engine.
  \item \textbf{Own customer data and relationships} --- order
        history, contact details, and re-engagement opportunities.
\end{itemize}
