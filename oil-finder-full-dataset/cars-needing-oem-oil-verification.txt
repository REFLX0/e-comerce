SPECPART.TECH — AUTOMOBILE MAKES WITHOUT A DEDICATED OIL SPEC
================================================================
Generated 2026-09-08 (v2, now with per-engine breakdown). Source:
backend/scripts/tecdoc-catalog-harvester.ts, function
deriveOilSpecification(), cross-checked against actual populated engines
in the live catalog DB. These makes have NO manufacturer-specific branch,
so every engine under them currently gets one of two generic fallback
specs instead of a real OEM spec:

  - Diesel, built 2011 or later: 5W-30, "Universal Low-SAPS DPF Compliant",
    ACEA C3, API SN/CF
  - Everything else: 5W-40, "Universal High-Performance", ACEA A3/B4,
    API SL/CF

Trucks (Iveco, DAF, MAN, Scania, etc.), motorcycles, marine, and
farm-tractor brands are NOT in this list -- they use separate datasets
already checked in an earlier pass. Pure-EV-only brands (Tesla, Fisker,
etc.) are also excluded since they carry no engine oil at all.

Each make below lists its real engine families as they exist in the
catalog: fuel type, displacement, and the year range they span, so you
(or your research agent) can look up era-correct specs instead of one
number for the whole brand. Where a make has more than 6 distinct
engine families, only the top 6 by volume are shown (labeled) -- the
rest are minor/rare variants.

Format for your answers, one block per ENGINE FAMILY (not just per
make) if the spec actually differs across eras/fuel types -- otherwise
one block per make is fine:
  MAKE (engine family, e.g. "essence 2000cc 1984-2005"):

  - API spec:
  - ACEA spec (or "none"):
  - OEM-specific oil name (or "none"):
